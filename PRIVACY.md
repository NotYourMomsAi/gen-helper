# Gen Helper Privacy

Gen Helper is a desktop app with no account and no server of its own.

## What stays on your PC

- Your library, boards, tags, people you add (including their photos), prompts, presets, settings and generated files.
- Your API keys, encrypted with your Windows account. They are only ever sent to the provider they belong to.

## What leaves your PC

Only the requests you make, sent straight from your PC to the service involved:

- **TVmaze** and **TMDB** (TMDB only with your own key): show, movie and people lookups.
- **Wikidata, Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons:** YouTube channel facts, summaries and photos.
- **YouTube** (only if you switch on the YouTube API with your own key): channel lookups.
- **Siray** and the **AI providers you connect**: the prompts, settings and reference files you choose to send.

Gen Helper collects no analytics and sends nothing to its maker.

**Phone and other devices** (off unless you switch it on in Settings): your PC serves the app's interface to devices on your local network that have opened your link. Nothing goes through any outside server; the link's code is the only key, and "New link" replaces it.

## YouTube API Services

With the YouTube API switched on, Gen Helper uses YouTube API Services. By using that feature you agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to the information YouTube receives. Data from YouTube is kept on your PC for at most 30 days (refreshed or removed), and is never sent to AI features. You can stop it at any time by switching the feature off or removing your key in Settings, or by deleting the key in Google Cloud.
